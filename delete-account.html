Delete your Bambo account

Bambo, by Bambo Tech

You can delete your account and all your data. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open Bambo and log in.
  2. Tap your profile, then the menu / Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm twice.

Option 2: on this page (no app needed)

Log in with your Bambo email and password to delete your account right now.

Option 3: you cannot log in

Email eltonmarumbwae@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Bambo account" and your username. We will confirm it is you and delete your account within 30 days.

Want to remove only some data (for example one reel or photo) and keep your account? You can delete your own posts and reels inside the app, or email us to remove specific data.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, phone number, bio and picture.
  • Your reels, photo posts, and the files you uploaded.
  • Your messages and conversations, including the copies the other person sees.
  • Your likes, follows and reel view records.
  • Your subscription record in Bambo and your login (email and password).

What we may keep

  • Backups that are overwritten automatically as they rotate. They are not restored to the live app.
  • Records Google Play keeps about your purchases, under Google's own policies.
  • Minimal records only if the law requires them.

Have Bambo Pro?

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play: Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Bambo Pro. Open your Google Play subscriptions.
