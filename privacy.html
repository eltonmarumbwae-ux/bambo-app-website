Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Bambo app ("Bambo", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Bambo is run by Bambo Tech in South Africa. By using Bambo you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy we need it
Account details: email address, password, full name, username and phone number you give when you sign up.To create your account, log you in and let people find you. Your password is handled by our login provider and stored in protected (hashed) form. We cannot read it.
Profile: bio, profile picture, and your privacy choices (for example hiding when you were last active).To show your profile.
Your content: reels (videos, thumbnails, captions), photo posts, and the messages you send, including group chat messages, photos, videos, voice notes and files.To store, show and deliver what you post and send.
Activity: who you follow, what you like, which reels you watched (used for view counts), people you block, reports you send about content or people, and when you were last active.To run the app, show view counts and follower lists, and show online status if you allow it.
Subscription details (only if you buy Bambo Pro): a purchase code from Google Play, and whether your subscription is active and when it ends.To confirm your subscription with Google and unlock longer reels. We never see your card or payment details. Google handles payment.
Coins, gifts and payouts: your coin purchases (Google Play order number and amount), gifts you send and receive, your diamonds, and, if you ask for a payout, a phone number or email we can reach you on.To add your coins, show gifts and earnings, pay creators, prevent fraud and keep financial records. We never see your card details; Google handles the payment. We may keep payment records (without your contact details) for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
Notification address: a code that identifies your phone for push notifications (only if you allow notifications).To send you notifications about messages, followers and likes. It is removed when you log out, switch notifications off in Settings, or delete your account.
Technical data: IP address and basic request information.Our hosting provider records this as normal for security and to keep the service running. App updates are delivered through Expo, which receives your app version and device type.

Stored only on your phone: comments you write, your app settings, the picture you chose as a profile photo, and (if you turn on fingerprint login) your saved login in your phone's secure storage. Your fingerprint itself is handled by your phone. We never receive it.

What we do not do: we do not show ads, we do not sell your personal information, and Bambo contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

2. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera: to take photos or videos to send in a chat.
  • Microphone: to record voice notes.
  • Photos, videos and files: when you choose something to post or send.

You can change these any time in your phone settings. Bambo works without them, but those features will not.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other Bambo users can see your username, name, profile picture, bio, and the reels and photos you post. The audio of a reel you post without music becomes an "original sound" that other people can use in their own reels. Messages are seen by the person (or people) you send them to.
  • Service providers that run Bambo for us: Supabase (login, database and server functions), Cloudflare (storing and delivering your videos, photos, voice notes and files) and Google (Google Play for subscriptions and app delivery). They process data only to provide those services. Your data may be stored on servers outside South Africa.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or to protect people from serious harm.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, posts, reels, messages, likes, follows, files and login (see how to delete your account). Copies in system backups are overwritten as backups rotate and are not restored to the live app. Google keeps its own purchase records under its own policies, and we may keep minimal records where the law requires it.

5. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and database access is limited by rules so you can only change your own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and turn on fingerprint lock if you want one.

6. Your rights

You can see and edit most of your details in the app (Settings). Under South African data protection law (POPIA) you may ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and object to how we use it. Email eltonmarumbwae@gmail.com. If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg.org.za.

7. Children

Bambo is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are under 18, use Bambo only with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. For big changes we will tell you in the app.
